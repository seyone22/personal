> **Pilot study (Data collected: November 18, 2026).** Daily supermarket prices were pulled from `food.seyone.dev` (`foodrepo`) across Cargills, Keells, Arpico, and SPAR, and benchmarked against Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) CCPI inflation releases up to October 2026.

In October 2026, the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) reported **8.3% year-on-year headline inflation** (CCPI, 2021=100) and **8.8% food inflation**, up from 2.2% headline inflation in March.

An aggregate number like **+8.8% food inflation** hides how uneven price changes feel on a weekly grocery run. To test whether automated retail tracking can serve as a real-time proxy for the official index, I ran a **pilot** comparing `food.seyone.dev` price histories against the Department of Census and Statistics (DCS) CCPI basket.

With **43 matched SKUs** covering **54.9% of the official CCPI food basket**, our pilot index lands at **`+9.25%`** from June to October 2026, right next to CBSL's **`+8.8%`**.

---

## Five Kitchen Essentials vs. CBSL Food Inflation

Before weighting the full basket, looking at five everyday Sri Lankan staples (**lime**, **green chili**, **coconut**, **masoor dhal**, and **rice**) shows why headline averages feel disconnected from fresh produce prices.

- **Lime (Cargills `250g`, normalized to `/kg`):** Over 63 days (Sep 16 to Nov 18), limes jumped **+54.5%** from **Rs. 1,152 / kg** (`Rs. 288` per 250g) to **Rs. 1,780 / kg** (`Rs. 445`), spiking to **Rs. 2,294 / kg** (`Rs. 573.50`) on Nov 16.
- **Green Chili (`1 kg`):** Across 222 valid daily observations since March 24, standard green chili swung up to **Rs. 2,100 / kg** before settling at **Rs. 550 / kg** today. On SPAR (150 days, Jun 18 to Nov 18), it ranged from **Rs. 245 / kg** to **Rs. 1,095 / kg** (**4.5x**), sitting at **Rs. 295 / kg** today.
- **Coconut (`1 unit`):** Up **+21.0%** on SPAR over 152 days (Jun 18 to Nov 18), climbing from **Rs. 157** (low of **Rs. 138**) to **Rs. 190**.
- **Masoor Dhal (`1 kg`):** Mostly flat or slightly down. SPAR Bulk moved from **Rs. 250** to **Rs. 239 / kg** (`-4.4%`), and Keells Red Dhal 1 kg dropped `-12.0%` (`Rs. 324` to `Rs. 285`) from March to November.
- **Rice (`Imported Keeri Ponni, 1 kg`):** Flat at **Rs. 255 / kg** (`0.0%` variance) across 77 tracked days (Aug 19 to Nov 4).

## Weighting by the Official CCPI (2021=100) Basket

To compare apples to apples, each SKU has to be weighted using the DCS CCPI (2021=100) expenditure shares from the 2019 Household Income and Expenditure Survey:

- **Food &amp; Non-Alcoholic Beverages:** `26.23%` of the total CPI basket (`Non-Food` is `73.77%`).
- **Rice (Samba, White/Red Kekulu, Nadu):** `2.72%` of total CPI (~**10.4%** of the food basket).
- **Milk Powder:** `1.71%` of total CPI (~**6.5%** of the food basket).
- **Coconut (nuts):** `1.38%` of total CPI (~**5.3%** of the food basket).
- **Sugar &amp; Dhal:** Sugar (`0.45%`) and Mysore Dhal (`0.50%`).
- **Individual fresh vegetables &amp; spices:** Typically `0.03%` to `0.35%` each (Big Onions `0.35%`, Beans `0.25%`, Chilli Powder `0.25%`, Lime `0.08%`).

Across **43 cleanly matched SKUs** (`14.41%` of total CPI, or **54.9%** of the `26.23%` food basket) with daily data from March 24 to November 18:

- **Jun 01 to Oct 31, 2026:** **`+9.25%`** (compared to CBSL's **`+8.8%`** October YoY food inflation).
- **Mar 24 to Oct 31, 2026:** **`+16.34%`**.
- **Mar 24 to Nov 18, 2026:** **`+21.22%`**, driven by an early-November jump in fresh vegetables, Red Onions (**+200.0%**, `Rs. 340` to `Rs. 1,020/kg`), and Big Onions (`+104.5%`).

## Item-for-Item Comparison Against CBSL Releases

Comparing our 43-item slice directly against the specific food drivers CBSL and DCS cited in September and October 2026 shows where they line up and where they diverge:

- **Matching upward drivers:** DCS flagged **limes**, **coconuts**, **big onions**, **red onions**, **milk powder**, **chilli powder**, and **normal bread** as primary September–October upward drivers. In `food.seyone.dev` through Oct 31, Cargills Lime was **+71.9%**, SPAR Coconut **+21.0%**, Big Onions **+95.5%** (`Mar→Oct`) / **+43.3%** (`Jun→Oct`), Red Onions **+94.1%** (`Mar→Oct`) / **+40.4%** (`Jun→Oct`), Anchor Milk Powder **+13.0%** (`Mar→Oct`) / **+8.7%** (`Jun→Oct`), Chilli Powder **+19.0%**, and Finagle Bread **+8.1%**.
- **Offsetting drops:** Keells Red Dhal (`-12.0%`), Red Kekulu Rice (`-8.3%`), Baby Carrots (`-34.1%`), and Baby Potatoes (`-9.1%`) declined in supermarket pricing, balancing out the sharper produce spikes.
- **Early-November lead:** Fresh vegetables like Beetroot (`+500.0%`), Cabbage (`+347.4%`), Bitter Gourd (`+281.8%`), Cucumber (`+250.0%`), and Red Onions (`Rs. 1,020/kg` by Nov 18) surged in early November, showing up in daily supermarket data weeks before CBSL's November CPI release.

## Current Coverage Gaps (The Remaining 45.1%)

Because this is a pilot built strictly on online supermarket listings, **45.1%** of the CCPI food basket is still unmapped or excluded:

- **Fresh fish (`~2.8%+`):** Keells and the tracked online supermarkets do not list fresh `Thalapath` (`0.84%`) or `Kelawalla` (`0.74%`) consistently online, only dry and canned fish (`0.0%` change).
- **Fresh chicken (`1.25%`) and fresh fruits:** Online product search either returned processed items (sausages, snacks, dehydrated fruit) or lacked clean 200-day series.

Closing those fresh-market gaps (especially fresh fish and fresh chicken) is the next step for `food.seyone.dev`, alongside automating unit normalization (`250g` packs vs. `1kg` bulk) across chains.
